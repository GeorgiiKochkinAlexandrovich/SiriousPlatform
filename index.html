FAQ

Frequently asked questions
What is Sirius Global?
Sirius Global is an online platform for fractional participation in UAE real-estate assets. After registration you can deposit USDT, purchase a Share Plan, optionally buy a Career License, invest in property shares, and claim income according to Platform rules.
What is the minimum deposit?
The minimum deposit is $240 USDT (TRC20). Enter the amount in the Deposit modal, send USDT to the displayed platform address, and submit a deposit request for verification.
How do deposits work?
Deposits are USDT on the TRON network only. After you submit a request, it appears as pending until our system or operations confirm the on-chain transfer. Your confirmed balance increases only after approval. Always double-check the wallet address and network before sending funds.
How long does deposit verification take?
Most deposits are reviewed within 24 hours after network confirmation. You can track pending items under Pending Deposit Requests in your Account cart tab.
What is a Share Plan?
A Share Plan sets the maximum total amount you can invest in property shares (for example Starter up to $5,000, up to Elite at $100,000). Plans are permanent. You can only upgrade by paying the difference; you cannot downgrade.
What is a Career License?
A Career License is a timed access product (1, 3, or 6 months). When the term ends, the purchase price is returned to your balance and you may buy again for any available term.
How do I buy property shares?
You need an active Share Plan and sufficient confirmed balance. On each property card, choose the number of shares (up to 10 per purchase), add to cart, and complete checkout. Share prices start from $170 on selected units and from $1,000 upward on others.
When can I claim income?
After purchase there is an initial unlock period (7 days), then you may claim daily income every 24 hours based on Moscow time timers shown in My Shares. Claimed income is credited to your confirmed balance.
How do withdrawals work?
Enter the USDT amount and your TRC20 address. A fixed fee of $8.50 USDT is added to every withdrawal. Your balance must cover amount + fee. After submission, the request is processed according to compliance and treasury procedures, typically within 24 hours of approval.
Do I need an account to browse properties?
Yes. The catalog, plans, and cabinet require sign-in. Privacy Policy, Terms, Licenses, and this FAQ remain readable without an account.
Is income guaranteed?
No. Figures shown are projections based on stated annual yields. Real outcomes depend on asset performance and Platform operations. Review the Terms of Use risk disclosure before investing.
How do I contact support?
Use the support channels published in your account area or on official Sirius Global communications. Include your registered email and relevant transaction IDs for faster handling of deposit or withdrawal questions.
